# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Syntax highlighting for IEC 61131-3 Structured Text (CODESYS / TwinCAT) and Siemens SCL.
- Guideline-based formatter (assignments aligned on column 41 by default) with a verifier that rejects any change other than whitespace and keyword casing.
- 13 lint rules for guideline violations.
- Outline, breadcrumbs and folding.
- ST and SCL snippets based on the guideline templates.
